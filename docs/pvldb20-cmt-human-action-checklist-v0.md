# PVLDB Volume 20 Human-Action Checklist

Verified against the official VLDB 2027 pages on 2026-09-22.

## Manuscript metadata

- [ ] Supply every author's full publication name in final order; use Latin characters consistently with CMT.
- [ ] Supply each author's institution, city, state/region if applicable, and country.
- [ ] Supply author email addresses and identify the corresponding author if the authors intend to designate one.
- [ ] Confirm whether any supplied ORCID should be included; none will be inferred.
- [ ] Confirm that the PDF title is exactly `CE-Replay: Executable Cardinality-Estimation Semantics for Statistics Physical Design`.
- [ ] Replace the explicit author placeholders and rebuild before upload.

## CMT metadata and timing

- [ ] Choose a monthly submission cycle and submit the mandatory abstract by 5:00 PM Pacific Time on the 25th of the preceding month.
- [ ] Submit the paper by 5:00 PM Pacific Time on the 1st; the final Volume 20 initial-submission deadline is March 1, 2027.
- [ ] Ensure every PDF author is registered in CMT using one consistent account.
- [ ] Enter title, abstract, author names, affiliations, and author order identically in CMT and the PDF.
- [ ] Select `Regular Research Paper`; do not append a special-category title suffix.
- [ ] Upload the author-complete PDF and provide the public supplemental repository URL: <https://github.com/1951123/ce-replay>.
- [ ] Confirm that the mandatory abstract and the PDF abstract are identical.

## Policy declarations

- [ ] Every author must declare all domain and individual conflicts of interest before the deadline.
- [ ] Nominate at least one qualified author-reviewer (normally an author with at least two prior SIGMOD, VLDB, ICDE, EDBT, or CIDR research papers); if none qualifies, nominate the best-qualified author for consideration.
- [ ] Declare every related concurrent submission and cite/mark it as under submission where the policy requires it.
- [ ] Confirm that the work is not simultaneously under review at another publication or presentation venue.
- [ ] Confirm whether any prior version, workshop paper, technical report, or preprint exists and review it against the originality policy.
- [ ] Confirm the per-author cap: no more than two research-track submissions in the chosen month and twelve in PVLDB Volume 20.
- [ ] Confirm that all listed authors approve the submission and ordering; authors cannot be changed after acceptance.

## Human declarations not inferable from this repository

The repository contains no authoritative author list, affiliations, CMT accounts, conflict declarations, reviewer nomination, submission-cycle selection, submission-cap status, or complete private publication history. Each therefore requires an explicit human declaration.
